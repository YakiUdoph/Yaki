Privacy by default

A temporary workspace, not a customer database.

Yaki keeps your imported file, review choices, and history in browser memory only. Refreshing or clearing the session removes them. The server does not store address requests.

No analytics, session replay, advertising scripts, external fonts, or hidden location requests are included.
